# Image Assets & Replacement Guide

You can easily replace any placeholder image on the website simply by dropping your own photo with the matching filename into the respective folder:

### 1. Hero / Portrait
- **Path**: `assets/images/portrait-hero.png`
- **Description**: Main cinematic monochrome hero portrait (already configured with your uploaded high-res photo).

---

### 2. Photography Portfolio (`assets/images/gallery/`)

- `events-optint.jpg` — OPTINT 2024 stage presentation / conference demo
- `astro-rohtang.jpg` — Milky Way & astrophotography over Rohtang Pass
- `documentary-outreach.jpg` — High-altitude / community screening documentary

### 4. Research & Innovation (`assets/images/research/`)

### 5. Honours & Medals (`assets/images/honours/`)

> **Note**: Standard `.jpg`, `.png`, or `.webp` files work seamlessly. Recommended aspect ratio for gallery photos is **4:5 (vertical)** or **16:9 (horizontal)**.
