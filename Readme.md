### `04` Research — SmartKC Case Study
- Deep-dive scientific case study on **SmartKC**: Smartphone-Based Corneal Topography for Keratoconus Detection.
- Highlighting recognition: **Winner — Best Scientific Model Demonstration** at **OPTINT 2024**.
- 5-Step Methodology breakdown:
  1. *Concept*: Mobile reflection optics overcoming diagnostic equipment cost in rural India.
  2. *Build*: 3D-built smartphone attachment with calibrated placido mires and diffuse ring illumination.
  3. *Imaging*: Tear-film reflection capture generating tangential and axial curvature maps.
  4. *Feasibility*: Camera variability, autofocus limitations, and Android compatibility analysis.
  5. *Outcome*: Validated proof-of-concept presented at OPTINT 2024.
- Curvature schematic SVG graphic and broader research interests matrix (Myopia control, Keratoconus, OCT & AI, Public health access).
